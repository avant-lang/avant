# Time limits of single test files in one suite pass, for tools/suite-limit.sh
# (tools/gate.sh): PATH ENV SECONDS REASON. ENV is the pass's environment,
# its words joined by `,` (`plain` for none). The pass's limit is the larger
# of SECONDS and the default (900 s); every other pass keeps the default.
tests/cases/test_fixed_point_verify.av AVANT_YOUNG_MB=1 1500 dumps the compiler under the verifier with a 1 MiB young generation, the pass's longest file: 206 s alone on the 32-thread server, 641 s while the verifier's checks fell back to whole ones (the pass then crossed 900 s under a final's load); its collections grow with the compiler
tests/cases/test_const_values.av AVANT_GC_STRESS=1 1800 the stress mode's longest file (a table built by one constructor, collected at every allocation): 317 s alone, 348 s in the final's pass beside another, past 900 s on a machine loaded to 70 or more runnable processes, where the old pass limit failed the whole pass
tests/cases/test_element_freeze.av AVANT_GC_STRESS=1 1800 the stress mode's second longest file: 313 s in the final's pass, the same load-driven spread as test_const_values
